# Instagram Comments Log

Tracking comments posted from @justhavealittlemeaning for organic reach growth.

## Guidelines (learned from experience)

- Comments in English (account is English-language)
- No emojis, no em dashes
- Short (1-2 sentences max), natural tone
- Must be consistent with our brand: psychology, self-reflection, therapeutic writing
- Avoid generic/abstract praise ("great post", "this is so true")
- Avoid overly long, intellectual comments
- Best approach: grounded, personal, relatable, but from the perspective of someone who works with self-reflection/journaling
- First comment on a post = great for visibility
